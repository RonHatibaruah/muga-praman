What is this Application?

This system provides End-to-End Digital Traceability & Non-Destructive Authentication for authentic Assam Golden Muga Silk (*Antheraea assamensis*) under the NESFIC-D-41 Challenge (Department of Handloom, Textiles & Sericulture, Govt. of Assam in partnership with AISF, DIIS, CMER&TI Lahdoigarh, and Central Silk Board).
