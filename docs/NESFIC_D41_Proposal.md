## Application Proposal Form: NESFIC-D-41
**Challenge Track:** Government Track (Dept. of Handloom, Textiles & Sericulture)  
**Problem Statement Title:** Digital Traceability and Non-Destructive Authentication of Muga Silk and Apparel from Cocoon to Consumer  
**Project Title:** Muga-Praman (মুগা-প্ৰমাণ): AI & Spectroscopic Traceability Engine for Assam Muga Silk  
**Principal Innovator:** Ron Hatibaruah  
**Target Beneficiaries:** 50,000+ Sericulture Rearers, 25,000+ Sualkuchi Master Weavers, Handloom Co-Operatives, and Global Luxury Silk Buyers  

---

### 1. Executive Summary
Assam Muga Silk (*Antheraea assamensis*) is an irreplaceable cultural and economic treasure of Assam, protected as a Geographical Indication (GI Certificate #38). However, the influx of cheap synthetic polyester and dyed Chinese Tasar imitations threatens the livelihoods of traditional artisans and erodes consumer trust. 

**Muga-Praman** delivers a complete digital provenance and non-destructive optical authentication system. By pairing field-portable Near-Infrared (NIR) spectrometers with cadastral GIS mapping and Layer-2 blockchain Merkle trees, the platform enables instantaneous purity testing without burning or cutting the fabric. Every genuine garment is stitched with a cryptographic GS1 Digital Link QR/NFC tag, connecting the end consumer directly to the Som tree plantation in Lakhimpur and the master weaver in Sualkuchi.

### 2. Department Alignment & Stakeholder Value
1. **Department of Handloom, Textiles & Sericulture:** Real-time visibility into statewide sericulture yields, cocoon bank arrivals, loom productivity, and anti-counterfeit enforcement.
2. **Central Muga Eri Research & Training Institute (CMER&TI):** Standardized digital protocol to deploy NABL accredited non-destructive testing directly to wholesale markets and retail emporiums.
3. **Smallholder Rearers:** Transparent shell ratio assessment at Cocoon Banks, eliminating middleman exploitation and securing Government Minimum Support Price (MSP).
4. **Sualkuchi Weavers:** Proof of authentic handloom origin, premium pricing in global luxury markets, and guaranteed artisan fair wages.
5. **Consumers & Retailers:** Instant leaf/cocoon-to-cloth provenance, official GI-38 seal, and Silk Mark validation via smartphone scan.

---

### 3. Technological Innovation & Key Differentiators
- **Non-Destructive Protein Fingerprinting:** Traditional chemical dissolution or burning tests ruin high-value apparel ($200–$800 USD). Our NIR spectroscopy evaluates secondary amide protein structures non-destructively in under 5 seconds.
- **Dharitree Cadastral GIS Integration:** Harvest coordinates are cross-referenced with official Assam ILRMS Dag & Patta records, ensuring sericulture land legitimacy.
- **Gas-Efficient Layer-2 Blockchain Provenance:** Merkle tree off-chain aggregation reduces gas costs by 99.8% while preserving cryptographic immutability.
- **Bilingual Offline-First Field Tools:** Assamese/English interface designed for ground-level rearers and reeling cooperatives.

### 4. Pilot Implementation Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Months 1–3):** Deploy pilot NIR spectrometers across Sualkuchi Silk Weaving Cluster and Dhakuakhana Cocoon Market; onboard 500 rearers and 200 master weavers.
- **Phase 2 (Months 4–6):** Integrate automated GS1 Digital Link QR tag dispensing at ARTFED and Pragjyotika emporiums in Guwahati, New Delhi, and Kolkata.
- **Phase 3 (Months 7–12):** Statewide rollout with Directorate Flying Squad anti-counterfeit inspection mobile kits.
